Common questions

Simple answers before you call.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes, estimates are always free. Call, text, or send a request with details of your project to get started. For most small jobs, we can quote right from photos you text us.

Which areas do you serve?

All of Southwest Florida: Naples, Marco Island, Bonita Springs, Estero, Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Golden Gate, and Lehigh Acres. A trip fee may apply for farther jobs, based on distance.

Can I send a list of small jobs?

Absolutely. A detailed punch list is a great way to get several home repairs addressed in one visit.

Are you insured?

Yes. Top 2 Bottom Home Solutions is insured.

Is there a minimum charge?

Yes, our minimum charge is $75. Estimates are always free, and you will know the price before any work begins.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, check, card, Zelle, and Cash App.

How quickly will I hear back?

We call back within 24 hours.

What work do you not do?

We do not do roofing, AC work, full remodels, or pool work. For jobs that need a permit or a specialized trade license, we coordinate with a licensed professional.
